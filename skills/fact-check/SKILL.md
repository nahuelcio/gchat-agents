---
name: fact-check
description: Verify specific claims against primary sources and rate each one. Use when asked whether something is true, to check a draft, or to validate numbers and quotes.
mcps: [exa]
---

## For each claim
1. Quote the claim exactly.
2. Find the primary source (official page, paper, filing, dataset, docs). Note its date.
3. Look for one independent confirmation or contradiction.
4. Rate it: true, mostly true, misleading, false or unverified.
5. Give the one-line reason and the URLs.

## Rules
- Prefer primary sources over news and summaries.
- Check whether a number or date is out of date.
- Say unverified rather than guessing.
