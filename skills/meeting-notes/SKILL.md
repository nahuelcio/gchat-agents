---
name: meeting-notes
description: Turn a transcript, chat thread or rough notes into clear minutes with decisions and action items. Use after meetings, for summaries of long threads, or for status updates.
---

## Output
1. One-paragraph summary.
2. Decisions made, each in one line.
3. Action items: owner, task, due date if stated.
4. Open questions and blockers.
5. Key discussion points only when they explain a decision.

## Rules
- Attribute decisions and tasks to the people who took them.
- Do not invent owners or dates; mark them as unassigned.
- Keep the speaker's meaning; drop small talk and repetition.
