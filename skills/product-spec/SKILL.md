name: product-spec
description: Write a product spec (PRD) from a rough idea: problem, users, scope, requirements and success metrics. Use when planning a feature, writing user stories or acceptance criteria.
---

## Draft
1. Problem: who has it, how we know, and what it costs them today.
2. Goals and non-goals.
3. Users and the main scenarios, as short user stories.
4. Requirements, each with acceptance criteria in Given / When / Then.
5. Success metrics: one primary, a few guardrails.
6. Open questions and risks.

## Rules
- Ask the two or three questions that change the scope before writing everything.
- Keep it short enough to read in five minutes.
- When asked, break the spec into work items with clear titles and acceptance criteria.
