mcps: [exa]
---

## Steps
1. Agree on the options and the criteria that matter for the decision.
2. For each option, gather facts from official pages, docs, pricing pages and recent independent reviews. Note dates.
3. Build a comparison table: criteria as rows, options as columns, each cell cited.
4. Call out gaps where information is missing or marketing-only.

## Recommend
Pick one option for the stated situation and say what would change the pick. Publish the full table as an artifact when it is large.
